FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Here are some common questions before getting started with Sovely.

Ask Your Question
What is dropshipping?

Dropshipping lets you sell products without keeping stock yourself. You list products, receive customer orders and place the fulfilment order with Sovely.

Do I need to maintain inventory?

No. Sovely is built around zero-inventory selling, so you don't need a warehouse or large upfront stock investment.

How does fulfilment work?

When you receive an order, you forward it through the system and Sovely handles packing, shipping and tracking.

How many products can I sell?

Sovely currently offers access to 10,000+ products across 25+ categories.

Can I sell on Amazon?

Yes. Sovely offers an Amazon Plan with account launch, listing, SEO, ads, FBA and related support.

Can beginners start?

Yes. The platform is designed to be simple enough for beginners while also supporting sellers who want to scale.
